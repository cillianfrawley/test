This website is a personal blog in the areas of Power and Energy, Dining, Adventures and
Literature. These areas combine to spell PEDAL, representing my favourite hobby: cycling. 

Cycling contains blog posts from both my personal cycling experiences, the world of 
professional cycling, cycling touring, technology and trips with friends.

Power and Energy contains any news, deep-dives, reports and projects about green energy 
and sustainability that I come across that I would like to share with others.

Dining contains a list of all my favourite recipes, cuisines sampled on holidays and other
dining related experiences.

Adventures represent any noteworthy travel, experiences and occasions.

Literature contains a database of books i've read, including ratings, summaries and
fun facts.
